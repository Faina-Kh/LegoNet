Source and modifications
------------------------

These files are derived from the Embrapa Wine Grape Instance Segmentation
Dataset (WGISD):

https://github.com/thsant/wgisd
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3361736

They combine WGISD grape-cluster bounding boxes with berry-point annotations
contributed by Faina Khoroshevsky and Stanislav Khoroshevsky. The annotations
were converted to the comma-separated train/validation/test inputs expected by
LegoNet and combined by image. The distributed files retain all 300 WGISD image
records; LegoNet performs the berry-subset and empty-box filtering at runtime.

The files should be attributed to the WGISD creators and to Faina Khoroshevsky
and Stanislav Khoroshevsky for the berry-point annotations. Cite the associated
paper:

F. Khoroshevsky, S. Khoroshevsky, and A. Bar-Hillel, "Parts-per-Object Count
in Agricultural Images: Solving Phenotyping Problems via a Single Deep Neural
Network," Remote Sensing, 13(13), 2496, 2021.
https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13132496
